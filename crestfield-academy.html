Welcome to Crestfield Lagos

Where Global Standards Meet African Excellence

Located on Admiralty Way in Lekki Phase 1, Crestfield International Academy was founded on the conviction that intellectual rigor and cultural character are inseparable. Our student body balances Cambridge international qualifications with Nigerian civic leadership, competitive football and swimming championships, and creative arts.

"We do not merely teach learners what to think; we cultivate in them the joyful curiosity, integrity, and resilience to lead on the global stage."
— Dr. Babatunde Vance, Principal & Head of Academy

Campus Grounds

World-Class Lekki Learning Facilities

Set along the peaceful Lekki waterfront, our campus unites state-of-the-art laboratories with lush green playing fields and residential halls.

Bannister STEM Innovation Center

Four advanced biochemistry laboratories, 3D additive printing suites, and automated electronics testbenches.

Wellington Memorial Library

Over 40,000 print volumes, peer-reviewed research databases, quiet study bays, and collaborative conference suites.

Blackwood Hall for the Arts

A 550-seat theater, black-box rehearsal studio, acoustic orchestral auditorium, and digital music recording room.

Pinnacle Athletics Complex

FIFA-regulation turf football pitch, 8-lane all-weather running track, 2 indoor basketball courts, and fitness center.

Aquatic Natatorium

Competition 25-meter heated swimming pool supporting school galas, water safety instruction, and inter-school tournaments.

The Palm Quadrangle & Boardwalk

Shaded waterfront study lawns, outdoor amphitheater for assembly, and secure, landscaped recreational spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Prospective Families

Key details on curriculum accreditations, bus routes, boarding, and admissions timelines.

We deliver an integrated dual curriculum. Students prepare for Cambridge International Examinations (Checkpoint, IGCSE, and Cambridge A-Levels) alongside the Nigerian National Curriculum (BECE and WAEC SSCE). This equips our graduates for both Nigerian university matriculation and admission into top universities across the UK, United States, and Canada.
Our monitored air-conditioned school bus fleet covers routes across Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Oniru, Chevron, Ikate, Ajah, and select Mainland pickup points (Ikeja GRA). All buses feature GPS tracking and onboard staff attendants.
Yes. For Junior and Senior Secondary learners (JSS 1 through SSS 3 / Sixth Form), Crestfield provides supervised modern residential boarding hostels separated by gender. The boarding experience includes evening prep, pastoral counseling, weekend sporting activities, and dedicated catering.
Class sizes are strictly capped at 15 learners per classroom, ensuring an average student-to-faculty ratio of 9:1. This guarantees individualized academic tracking, mentoring, and support.
